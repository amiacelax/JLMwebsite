# Daiki — English business vocab & phrases

Teacher handout for dried-fruit / dry-snack business English (mangoes, nuts, almonds, gift sets, exports).

---

## Five useful English business words

### 1. Inventory
The stock of goods you have.

**Example:** "We need to check our inventory of dried mangoes before the next shipment."

### 2. Supply Chain
The full process of getting raw materials, producing, and delivering products.

**Example:** "Our supply chain for nuts has been delayed due to weather issues."

### 3. Revenue
The total money a company makes from sales (before costs).

**Example:** "Exporting to Europe helped increase our revenue this quarter."

### 4. Brand
The image and reputation of your company's products.

**Example:** "We should strengthen our brand as a healthy, premium dry fruit snack."

### 5. Margin (often "profit margin")
The difference between selling price and cost.

**Example:** "We need to improve our profit margin on the mixed fruit packs."

---

## Five useful phrases

### 1. "Could you please clarify...?"
**Example:** "Could you please clarify the delivery schedule for next month?"

### 2. "I suggest we..."
**Example:** "I suggest we try a new packaging design for the gift sets."

### 3. "Let's discuss the next steps."
**Example:** "The meeting went well. Let's discuss the next steps for the Korean market."

### 4. "We're running low on..."
**Example:** "We're running low on almonds, so we should place a new order soon."

### 5. "How does that sound to you?"
**Example:** "We can offer a 10% discount for orders over 500kg. How does that sound to you?"

---

## Practice: five combined phrases

Use a business word **inside** a useful phrase — speak or email these as full lines.

### 1. Inventory + clarify
**Could you please clarify our inventory of dried mangoes before we confirm the next shipment?**  
ドライマンゴーの在庫を、次の出荷を確定する前にはっきり教えていただけますか？

### 2. Supply chain + suggest
**I suggest we review our supply chain for nuts after these weather delays.**  
この天候による遅延のあと、ナッツのサプライチェーンを見直すことを提案します。

### 3. Revenue + next steps
**Exports to Korea went well. Let's discuss the next steps to grow our revenue further.**  
韓国への輸出はうまくいきました。さらに売上（レベニュー）を伸ばす次のステップについて話し合いましょう。

### 4. Brand + how does that sound
**We could strengthen our brand as a healthy, premium dry fruit snack with new gift-set packaging. How does that sound to you?**  
新しいギフトセットの包装で、ヘルシーでプレミアムなドライフルーツ・スナックとしてのブランドを強化するのはどうでしょう？ いかがですか？

### 5. Margin + running low
**We're running low on almonds, and that could squeeze our profit margin if we rush a pricey last-minute order.**  
アーモンドの在庫が少なくなっています。急ぎの高値発注になると、利益率（マージン）が圧迫されるかもしれません。
