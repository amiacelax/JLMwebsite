Something worth knowing

A careful appraisal can protect your family’s money, too.

When a home passes through an estate, the appraisal helps set a new cost basis. That number matters later—especially if the house is sold. Get it wrong, or skip it, and families sometimes pay far more in taxes than they needed to.

I’ve seen a few hundred dollars spent on an appraisal protect a family from roughly $10,000 they would have paid unnecessarily. It’s not something to rush past— and it’s not something you have to untangle alone.
