# Off-Site SEO Checklist for JEM Appraisals

Do these in the same week you publish the website. The site helps people who find you; these steps help people **find** you in the first place.

---

## 1. Google Business Profile (highest priority)

- [ ] Go to [https://business.google.com](https://business.google.com) and create or claim a profile.
- [ ] **Business name:** JEM Appraisals
- [ ] **Category:** Real estate appraiser (add secondary categories if offered, e.g. Real estate consultant)
- [ ] **Service area:** Deale, Anne Arundel County, Maryland (or address if he uses a home office and is comfortable listing it)
- [ ] **Phone:** 301-335-1105
- [ ] **Website:** Your Netlify URL (e.g. `https://jem-appraisals.netlify.app`)
- [ ] **Hours:** Set to “by appointment” or actual hours
- [ ] **Description:** Use plain language from the website — 40+ years, estate/probate, VA, expert witness, Deale MD
- [ ] **Photos:** Add the same headshot as the website when ready; exterior/office optional
- [ ] **Verify** the listing (Google will send a postcard, phone, or email option)

**Tip:** Ask your dad to respond to any Google reviews within a few days — it helps visibility.

---

## 2. Bing Places for Business

- [ ] Go to [https://www.bingplaces.com](https://www.bingplaces.com)
- [ ] Enter the same business name, phone, website, and service area as Google
- [ ] Claim and verify the listing

---

## 3. Ask for reviews (after completed jobs)

- [ ] Identify 2–3 trusted clients (estate attorneys, executors, repeat customers)
- [ ] After a successful appraisal, ask: “Would you mind leaving a short Google review? It helps other families find us.”
- [ ] Do not offer payment or incentives for reviews (against Google policy)

---

## 4. Consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone)

Use the **exact same** spelling everywhere:

| Field | Value |
|-------|--------|
| Name | JEM Appraisals |
| Phone | 301-335-1105 |
| Email | houseappraiser@comcast.net |
| Location | Deale, MD |

Match this on the website, Google, Bing, any attorney referral pages, and business cards.

---

## 5. Optional — low effort, later

- [ ] List on Maryland or Anne Arundel professional directories if free listings exist
- [ ] Ask estate planning attorneys if they maintain a “preferred vendors” list
- [ ] Ensure any existing professional licenses list a contact number matching the site

---

## What to expect

- New sites take **weeks to months** to rank for competitive terms.
- **“Estate appraisal Deale MD”** and similar long, specific searches may show results sooner.
- Google Business Profile often drives **phone calls** before the website ranks on its own.
- Keep the site live, profile verified, and NAP consistent — avoid creating duplicate Google listings.

---

## When you buy a domain

Update the website URL on Google and Bing to the new domain (e.g. `https://jemappraisals.com`). No need to rebuild the site — only DNS and the few URL fields in `index.html`, `robots.txt`, and `sitemap.xml`.
