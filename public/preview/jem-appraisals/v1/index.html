Here’s the trouble

Skip a real appraisal, and you can pay for it later.

Say the home was bought decades ago for a fraction of today’s value. Without a proper appraisal, the cost basis may not reflect what the property was actually worth when it passed to the heirs. Sell later, and you may pay taxes on a bigger gain than you should have.
