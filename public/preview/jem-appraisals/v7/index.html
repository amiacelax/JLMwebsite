Why do I need an appraisal?

Let’s say your parents bought decades ago for a fraction of today’s value. You don’t want that old price stuck as the cost basis—you want today’s value. Sell without an appraisal, and you might owe thousands more in taxes.

I’ve seen families spend just $500 for an appraisal that saves them thousands in unnecessary taxes.

Is it too late?

Not always. I specialize in retrospective appraisals, where we appraise the property according to a past date—exactly what many estate/probate cases need.
