Japanese Jumpstart

5-day conversation roadmap

A focused sprint for getting useful Japanese into your mouth quickly, with each day building toward a real conversation.

Day 1

Introduce yourself thoroughly

Name, background, why you are learning, what you like, and how to sound natural instead of textbook-stiff.

Day 2

Who, what, when, where, why, how

Build question patterns so you can ask for information, keep a conversation moving, and understand what people ask you.

Day 3

Actions and daily routines

Use core verbs to explain what you do, what you did, what you want to do, and what you are planning next.

Day 4

Needs, preferences, and opinions

Say what you like, want, need, think, and feel so your Japanese starts expressing your actual personality.

Day 5

Put it all together

Guided conversation practice using the whole week: introductions, questions, answers, follow-ups, and repair phrases.
