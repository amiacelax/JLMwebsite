What is Homework Hub?

A subscription for learners who want expert-made homework and language advice without booking a full private lesson every week. After each lesson recording, you get short practice that matches what we did — plus access to your lesson video when it’s ready.

  • Homework drafted from your lesson (AI-assisted, always reviewed by JD before you see it)
  • Fill-in-the-blank style sheets in the browser — type answers like a PDF form
  • Account types: Current Student (private-lesson students) or Homework Only
  • Three paid tiers — Basic, Premium, and Ultra — plus a Student Special Tier for current students
  • Personal video feedback from JD is included on Ultra only — not sold as a separate add-on
  • Discord ping when homework and YouTube lesson links are ready
  • Pitch accent checker coming soon
