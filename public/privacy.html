Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Japanese Language Mentor (“JLM”, “I”, “me”) runs japaneselanguagementor.com and the Homework Hub. This page explains what information is collected, why, and how you can ask for changes or deletion.

What I collect

  • Account info — email (login), first/display name (for “Alex’s Hub”), and a hashed password. An internal username may still exist for older accounts and teacher tools.
  • Homework data — answers, submissions, teacher notes, notebooks, and related media you upload.
  • Messages you send — contact forms, promo signup, feature/bug reports (bug reports may include a screenshot).
  • Payment status — plan tier after you confirm PayPal checkout (PayPal handles card details; I do not store full payment card numbers).
  • Browser storage — login session and a few preferences (theme, recent worksheets) in localStorage / sessionStorage. These are functional, not advertising cookies.

How it’s used

  • To run your Homework Hub account and show your work to JD for review.
  • To notify JD (currently via Discord) when homework is submitted or you send feedback.
  • To email you only if you opted into updates (promo / “coming soon” lists).
  • To improve the site and fix bugs.

Where it’s stored

Site and homework data are hosted on Cloudflare (Workers + KV / assets). Discord is used for teacher notifications. PayPal processes subscriptions. I do not sell your personal data.

Your choices (GDPR-style rights)

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, or delete your personal data. In Homework Hub you can update your display name, change your password, log out, or delete your account under Account. You can also email me via the contact form.

Children

Homework Hub is aimed at learners and adults studying Japanese. If you believe a child under 16 created an account without a parent/guardian’s involvement, contact me and I’ll help remove it.

Changes

I’ll update this page when practices change. Continued use of the site after updates means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions: use the contact form on the homepage.
